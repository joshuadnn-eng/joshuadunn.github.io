Three posts a week. One look.

Consistency builds trust. I set the visual direction, the post ideas and the calendar, and our designers brought them to life in Adobe. Every Monday, Wednesday and Friday, the feed showed up looking like one brand.

What it taught me

The pop-up made the sales. The screen didn't. When we went fully online in March 2023, sales stopped, and by 2024 the funding had too. Now I never switch off a channel that's working until the new one has proven itself, and I keep a real-world touchpoint while a brand grows online.

Marketing & Google Ads strategy

No website yet, and no budget to waste. I planned how a Krugersdorp repair shop turns a brand-new site into phone calls and WhatsApps: start small and local, track every lead, and only spend more once the numbers say so.

Case study · 2020 – now

SneakerVaultSA

Founder · Strategy, production & partnerships

A sneaker and streetwear media brand I started from zero and still run on my own. Every part of it is a marketing job: choosing the audience, planning the calendar, making the content, reading the numbers and selling partnerships to brands.

  • 1M+organic views across long-form and short-form video
  • 2.5Mviews on a single short-form video
  • 2.8Mshort-form views in one 90-day period
  • 17.6Khours of watch time
  • ~10paid pieces for Shesha, after I pitched them
  • 10+brands partnered with, from ASICS to Superbalist

Run like a business, not a hobby.

The same playbook I'd bring to your brand, tested with my own name on it.

  • Know the audience92% of my viewers are men and 76% are 18 to 34, so every shoe, price point and title is chosen for sneaker buyers in that bracket.
  • Win the searchOver half of my short-form views come from search, not the feed. So titles, covers and descriptions are written around what people type, like prices and "where to buy".
  • Package for the clickOne recognisable house style for every thumbnail and cover, designed in versions and refined on results.
  • Sell the partnershipI pitched Shesha a review idea and became their repeat creator for about six months. I've since delivered work for ASICS, Superbalist, Converse, Reebok and Puma, and a branded brief for Russian Bear × YFM.
  • Run the operationMy own content calendar, my own pricing and packages, and every shoot, edit and motion graphic done in-house.

What it proves

In October 2026 I relaunched with a new thumbnail style and a heavier short-form schedule. The next 28 days brought 30,000 views, 21,800 more than usual. I don't just recommend tactics. I test them, measure them and keep what works.

Made in-house

I shoot, edit and animate my own work, so I can brief designers and editors in their own language.
